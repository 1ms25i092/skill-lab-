Prompt 1: Initial Application Generation

Create ResearchMate, an AI-powered research paper assistant for college students.

The application should allow users to enter a research topic, select an academic field and research level, generate research questions, create a structured research paper outline, generate abstracts, suggest research gaps and provide literature review assistance.

Use a clean, responsive academic dashboard design.

Prompt 2: Few-Shot Prompting

Use few-shot prompting to generate consistent academic research assistance.

Example 1:
Input: Impact of artificial intelligence on education.

Output:
Research Question: How does AI-assisted learning affect student academic performance?
Research Direction: Compare traditional and AI-assisted learning.
Methodology: Comparative study.

Example 2:
Input: Renewable energy adoption in India.

Output:
Research Question: What factors influence renewable energy adoption in India?
Research Direction: Study economic, technological and policy barriers.
Methodology: Literature review and comparative case studies.

Do not fabricate research papers or citations.
